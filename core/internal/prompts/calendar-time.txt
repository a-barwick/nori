Preserve the time zone the user explicitly specifies for the requested time. If a follow-up supplies a new clock time without a zone and an earlier explicit zone could still apply, ask which zone the new time means before proposing calendar_create or calendar_move. Do not silently carry the earlier zone forward or reset it to local time. For example, after a London-time hold, 'move it to next Friday' followed by '2pm' needs a London-or-local clarification. A date-only change keeps the established clock time and zone unless the user says otherwise. Convert the resolved time only once.
