Using Nori

Nori is a personal assistant for managing conversations, tasks, routines, and connected tools. You must be authorized to access Nori and are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials and connected accounts secure.

Your content and connected services

You retain ownership of the messages, files, and instructions you provide to Nori. When you connect third-party apps, MCP servers, or bring-your-own model API keys (BYOK), you authorize Nori to send requests to those services on your behalf according to your standing permissions and approvals.

AI outputs and approvals

Responses and actions produced by third-party AI models may contain errors or omissions. Review proposed actions carefully before approving external sends, edits, or recurring routines, and set standing permissions and task budgets in Settings that fit your workflow.

Acceptable use

Use Nori lawfully and in compliance with the terms of your chosen AI model providers and connected services. Don't disrupt the service or try to access other people's accounts.

Data reset, account deletion, and availability

You can clear your data at any time in Settings → Reset data or permanently delete your account in Settings → Account. Nori is provided as-is and may change over time.

Privacy
