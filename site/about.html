About Nori

A personal assistant for the web, iPhone and Mac.

Nori is a personal assistant you talk to like a person. Ask it to write an email, plan around your calendar, read a document or look something up, and it does the work while you get on with your day.

What Nori can do

  • Email: write and send email from your Gmail, after you approve each one.
  • Calendar: find free time, and create or move events on your Google Calendar.
  • Documents: read and edit the Google Docs, Sheets and PDFs you choose.
  • Research: search the web and save what it finds, such as a comparison table, to your library.
  • Reminders and routines: remind you later, or send you something on a schedule, like a Monday summary of your week.
  • Memory: remember your preferences and the details you share, in a list you can read and edit.

Your choice of AI model

Nori can use an AI model running on your own Mac or Linux computer, or your own account with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or OpenRouter.

Where your data lives

Nori's servers run on Google Cloud in the United States, and your data is encrypted there. The Privacy Policy explains what Nori stores and how to delete it.

Status

Nori is in private beta, and sign-in works for invited accounts. Its source code is on GitHub.
