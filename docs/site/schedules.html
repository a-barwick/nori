Proactive

Your appointment ends at 1:45, and the meeting is 24 minutes away. Want me to look for another time?

Nobody asked. Built as the calendar check.

The one timer that runs today

Cloud Scheduler calls the calendar check at 12 am and 12 pm Pacific time. Reminders and recurring tasks the user asks for have no timer yet.

Task results aren't offers

A result that arrives later belongs to a request the user made. It's always saved, and it doesn't count against the daily cap on offers.
