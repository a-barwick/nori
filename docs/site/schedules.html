Schedules

Reminders, later tasks and monitors the user asks for run at their time with no new message.

Outer loopAsks the runtime to save “Stretch at 3 p.m. every weekday”.
↓ saved with the reply
RuntimeSaves the rule and asks Cloud Tasks to call it at 3 pm.
↓ due at 3 pm
RuntimeAdds “Time to stretch.” to the conversation, after any reply in progress.
↓ reminder text, if no tab showed it in 90 seconds
GmailEmails the reminder to the user from the connected account.

Three kinds

KindExampleWhen it's due
ReminderTake the laundry out in 20 minutes.The runtime adds the reminder text as its own message.
TaskTomorrow at 7 am, check the weekend weather and suggest a picnic.A task starts with the saved instructions and looks things up then.
MonitorTell me if the black 1 TB laptop drops below $600. Check daily for two weeks.A read-only task checks. The user hears nothing unless the condition is met.

A monitor ends at its end date, or when a check reports that the user's stopping point was reached. Each check gets the list of what was already reported, so the same news isn't sent twice.

Repeats and changes

Repeats

  • Once, daily, weekdays or chosen weekdays
  • A day of the month, or its last business day
  • Every few hours
  • US federal holidays, when the user names that calendar

Changes from chat

  • Skip one date, such as next Wednesday
  • Snooze to a later time, once
  • Pause, resume or cancel
  • Run once now

Times stay at the user's local time. A daily 8 am reminder is still 8 am after the clocks change.

The web client's activity panel also lists open schedules, each with Pause or Resume and Cancel buttons.

Missed and late runs

  • An occurrence more than 30 minutes late covers every time that passed, so it runs once.
  • A late reminder is still delivered, with a line saying when it was due.
  • A late task doesn't run. The runtime posts a notice, and the task runs once if the user asks.
  • If Cloud Tasks never calls the runtime at the due time, the runtime's twice-daily calendar check runs any due schedules, so none waits more than 12 hours.

Email when the app is closed

Reminders, and replies to task results and task questions, wait 90 seconds for an open tab to show them. A visible tab reports the newest reply it shows, and then no email goes out.

  • Quiet hours, 10 pm to 7 am by default, hold emails until they end.
  • Once 8 emails have gone out in a day, the rest are dropped. Reminders still go.
  • Reminders skip quiet hours and the daily limit.
