Roadmap

Twelve milestones, in order. A milestone is done when it works on noribot.chat against its scenarios.

  • Linear holds the twelve milestones. Milestones 5 to 12 are planned.
  • We measure progress against 106 written conversation scenarios, scoring outcome, context, grounding, control and interaction separately.
  • The bars show Linear issue completion. Milestones 3 and 4 are deployed, and milestone 5 isn't.
  • Milestone 4's acceptance remains open. The runtime marked a finished task incomplete after the Jev classifier returned low confidence (AB-235).

What milestone 4 changed

  • Tasks finish with an explicit complete call and a completeness check from the Jev classifier.
  • Tool errors go back to the model.
  • Reads can be corrected within limits. Failed tasks and uncertain writes wait for user direction.
  • History moved out of workflow payloads.

Planned, with no Linear issue of its own

  • Email and document tools. The first planned connector is the calendar.
  • Reusable procedures learned from feedback. AB-156 treats these as a later extension.
  • Taking over the computer by hand, and concurrent browser access. AB-139 treats both as later work.
Behavior the milestones have to show
  • Ordinary conversation creates no task, and a mixed message answers and dispatches.
  • A three-message burst produces one current reply.
  • A restart preserves messages and task IDs, and retrying an ID does nothing.
  • Two tasks progress separately, and a correction, question, budget pause or Stop in one leaves the other alone.
  • A question survives reload, pause and restart.
  • An uncertain write is resolved without a duplicate.
  • Subtask outcomes resume their parent, costs roll up, and stopping a parent includes subtasks.
  • Saved results reopen after a disconnect, and status questions don't rerun actions.
  • Chat works while task execution is paused.
