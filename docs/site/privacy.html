Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Information We Collect

When you sign in to Nori, we store your email address, display name, timezone, and the conversations, tasks, memory entries, preferences, and files you create or upload.

Model API keys you provide (BYOK) are encrypted at rest with Google Cloud KMS and tenant-scoped encryption keys before storage.

Third-Party Model Providers

When you send a message or run a task, Nori forwards the necessary conversation context and files to the AI model provider you selected using your configured API key. Your provider's privacy policy governs how they process those requests.

Telemetry and Diagnostics

Nori collects error diagnostics and performance telemetry (such as Sentry and Arize traces) with sensitive credentials and user content redacted, used solely to keep the service reliable.

Retention, Data Reset, and Account Deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can reset your Nori data or permanently delete your entire account at any time from Settings → Account in the Web or iOS app.

Permanent account deletion removes your user directory record, conversations, tasks, memory, leases, encrypted API keys, and stored files from our database and cloud storage.
