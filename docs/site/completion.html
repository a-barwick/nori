What this catches

What's the weather in Phoenix? Actually, Tucson.

An answer about Phoenix can't be published, because the instructions changed while it was being written.

Did you send it?

Nori answers from the receipt, or checks with the provider. A status question never sends anything again.

  • The check covers completeness. It doesn't prove every fact in the answer is true.
