How we measure progress

There are 106 written conversations in 16 categories, using 223 user messages and 12 writing styles. They run from “hi” to corrections, changes of mind and long-running work.

Each one is scored on five things separately:

  • Outcome: did the requested answer, artifact, action or schedule result?
  • Context: were references, dates, corrections and people resolved correctly?
  • Grounding: are claims backed by available evidence?
  • Control: were scope, cancellation, approval and privacy limits respected?
  • Interaction: did the reply fit the requested voice, format and level of detail?
