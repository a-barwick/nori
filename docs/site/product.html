How we measure progress

There are 106 written conversations in 16 categories, using 223 user messages and 12 writing styles. They run from “hi” to corrections, changes of mind and long-running work.

Each one is scored on five things separately:

  • Outcome: did the right thing happen?
  • Context: did Nori keep track of what was said?
  • Grounding: are its claims backed by something real?
  • Control: did Austin stay in charge?
  • Interaction: did it feel natural?
