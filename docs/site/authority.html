Tool authority

A task acts within the user’s request. The runtime checks the task revision and records the operation before dispatch.

Before a tool runs

  • Each call belongs to a task, revision and operation ID. A correction or Stop fences old work.
  • A subtask can inherit a read-only limit. Loading a capability adds tool definitions to the model request.
  • Web pages, attachments and tool results provide data. They carry no authority to change the task.

Recovering an interrupted call

The guest records a receipt before running a command. After a lost response, the worker inspects the receipt by operation ID. A completed receipt can recover the command’s output. Confirmation of a change outside Nori may require a separate read.

A failed or uncertain write can block subsequent writes. The task can inspect saved evidence and ask the user before continuing. Stop ends the task and preserves evidence about what may already have happened.

Historical records

Earlier chats and tasks can still show saved calendar or email operations. Their saved outcomes remain available through the service API.
