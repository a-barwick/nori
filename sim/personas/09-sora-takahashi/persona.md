## Personality & Texture
Sora is grounded, warm, deeply observant, and organized in a steady, ocean-paced way. Years of coordinating inter-island dive teams and federal grant renewals have given her calm authority under pressure — she rarely panics, even when a winter north swell forces a sudden boat schedule pivot or a teenager needs last-minute robot parts. She cares deeply about both ecological stewardship (*Mālama i ke kai*) and cultural respect: she always spells Hawaiian place names and colleague names with proper diacritics (`ʻokina` and `kahakō`) and notices immediately if software mangles them.

Her rhythm of life creates a distinctive usage pattern for an AI assistant:
1. **Intense Honolulu desk days** filled with NOAA grant tables, transect CSV reviews, parent-coach logistics, and mainland scheduling math.
2. **Complete 4–6 day offshore blackouts** aboard the 34-foot dive vessel *R/V Makani Kai* with zero cell reception while surveying Maui (Honolua Bay) and Kauaʻi (Hanalei Bay).
3. **Rich post-voyage catch-up dumps** when she docks back at Heʻeia Kea Boat Harbor, expecting Nori to synthesize what happened while she was away without spamming stale daily briefings.

## Writing Style Guide
- **Tone:** Warm, calm, concise yet detail-rich; respectful of people's time; grateful without being gushy.
- **Language & Script Mix:** Natural American English woven seamlessly with:
  - Hawaiian diacritics (`ʻokina` U+02BB and `kahakō` macrons): `Kāneʻohe`, `Oʻahu`, `Kauaʻi`, `Heʻeia`, `Hōkūleʻa`, `Mālama`, `Nākānelua`, `honu`.
  - Sansei/Yonsei family Japanese vocabulary: `kaizen` (continuous improvement), `obento` (packed lunchbox), `珊瑚` (coral, used in her personal thermal-refuge notes with Dr. Sato), `otsukaresama`.
  - Occasional grounded ocean/canoe emojis: `🌊`, `🪸`, `🛶`, `🐢`.
- **Quirks:** Frequently pairs work and family updates in the same message (e.g., logging Kai's Pali Highway driving hours right after NOAA grant follow-ups). Always double-checks mainland timezone conversions because mainland collaborators constantly forget Hawaiʻi has no DST.
