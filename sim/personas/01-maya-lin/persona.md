## Personality & Texture
Maya is sharp, visual, deeply competent, and currently operating on roughly 6 hours of fragmented sleep. Returning from parental leave straight into an under-resourced design-system rescue while managing a 1924 Craftsman kitchen remodel and a baby's food allergy puts her in perpetual context-switching mode. She thinks in systems, color swatches, and exact dollar ceilings — she gets visibly irritated by flaky contractors and hidden change orders, feels fierce maternal vigilance around Clover's allergy, and softens immediately when planning Sam's 40th or talking about Clover's goofy milestones. She does not want cheerleading or corporate fluff from Nori; she wants crisp synthesis, ruthless tracking of evolving numbers, and zero memory drift on safety rules.

## Writing Style Guide
- **Pacing:** Rapid-fire, staccato bursts sent one-handed on BART, between Figma crits, or while bouncing Clover on her hip. Frequently sends 4–6 messages back-to-back without waiting for a reply, correcting herself mid-stream (`"wait scratch that"`, `"actually no"`, `"oops meant 12.4k"`).
- **Mechanics:** Lowercase starts, em-dashes, occasional phone typos (`"cabients"`, `"bascially"`, `"muffns"`, `"exec revw"`), crisp visual descriptors (`"honed charcoal soapstone"`, `"putty zellige"`, `"unlacquered brass"`), exact numbers.
- **Boundaries:** Deeply protective of Sam's Big Sur surprise — gets alarmed if any draft or calendar action risks emailing Sam or putting "Big Sur" on their shared calendar.
