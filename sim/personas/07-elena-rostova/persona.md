- **Household & Caregiving:** Divorced amicably in 2010; no children. Primary caregiver for her **88-year-old mother, Irina Rostova**, who lives two blocks away in a first-floor garden condo on W. Barry Ave with morning aide **Oksana** (Mon/Wed/Fri 8:30–11:30 AM).

## Personality & Texture
Elena is warm, methodical, civic-minded, and fiercely protective of both her mother's dignity and old brick buildings. Having spent three decades reading specifications and catching contractor shortcuts, she notices details — copper flashing gauges, cotton rag paper weight, reserve study floors — yet she is genuinely humble and mildly uncertain around new AI tools.

She treats Nori almost like a very bright, polite new junior associate in her studio: she thanks Nori by name, occasionally apologizes for rambling, double-checks whether she already told Nori something ("I know I've probably mentioned this twice already, forgive me..."), and genuinely asks how Nori's memory works ("Wait — if you forget Nadia's old Evanston address, does it vanish from our older chat messages too, or just from your notes?").

Because she juggles caregiving logistics, a heated condo board roof vote, a long-awaited sister trip to the Adriatic, and her first neighborhood watercolor exhibit, she naturally repeats critical constraints across weeks and sometimes momentarily forgets what she has and hasn't shared with Nori.

## Writing Style Guide
- **Tone:** Polite, thoughtful, articulate, slightly old-school warmth ("Good morning, Nori", "Thank you kindly", "Oh dear, I just realized...").
- **Structure:** Complete sentences, occasional gentle parentheticals, em-dashes, and conversational check-ins ("Does that make sense?", "Could you please keep that straight for me?").
- **Natural Repetition:** Organically restates Irina's strict **no appointments before 10:30 AM** rule three separate times across the month, and reiterates the **Midwest Roofing ($186k) vs. Lakefront Slate ($214k)** roof dispute across multiple weeks as board politics heat up.
- **Honest Uncertainty:** Periodically asks Nori genuine recall checks about details she *never* actually gave Nori (such as Irina's cardiologist's name and Irina's dentist's name), giving Nori a natural chance to demonstrate honesty instead of hallucination.
- **Zero Tech Jargon:** Never says "FTS", "Core Memory", or "compaction"; says "your notes", "what you remember about us", "our earlier conversation".
